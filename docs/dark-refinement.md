# Boundary Studies — dark architectural refinement

## Audit before editing

The live production homepage, research archive, and phone composition were captured and inspected before source edits. Seven content routes were captured at 390 and 1440px, including rotated/sectioned sculpture and permitted request states. Evidence: .qa/refinement/before/.

The existing monumental TRUST/BREAKS typography, asymmetric folios, substantive articles, native navigation, disclosure wording, and caller/policy model are preserved. The audit found light backgrounds in body/page introductions/folios/recognition/contact/article code blocks; olive/lime tokens and hardcoded illustration/status colors; a mint rounded favicon; circular request nodes, rounded switches and loading markers; lowercase r/h in the home identity print; and curved membranes throughout artwork. Motion was mostly short vertical entries, with room for coordinated geometry and material lighting.

## Direction and implementation

Obsidian surfaces, graphite depth, cold platinum text, restrained electric blue, and a secondary violet spectral edge. Zero-radius controls and illustrations. The trust sculpture becomes a projected stack of rectangular architectural plates, retaining rotation, separation, and cross-section controls. Its illuminated policy plane gives the fictional security boundary a physical structure. The same angular vocabulary connects request diagrams, note illustrations, monogram, methodology, and social artwork.

Colors are migrated in the source design system; section classes describe dark surfaces. Amber DENY indicators retain clear semantic meaning; blue PERMIT still has explicit textual feedback. Entry choreography, directional edge scans, pointer lighting, and scroll-linked structural movement are restrained and cleaned up. Native scrolling, visible content without JavaScript, keyboard equivalents, reduced motion, and phone-specific diagrams remain requirements.

## Verification

The source audit, first rendering pass, second refinement pass, and final production review are complete. Captures are saved in `.qa/refinement/{before,pass-1,pass-2,final}/`. Dark tokens now cover every route, mobile menu, footer, article code panel, error interface, pending indicator, favicon, and social artwork. All custom interface geometry uses zero radius; square nodes and angular plate/path illustrations replace circular and curved decorations. Amber warning/deny semantics remain explicit.

Actual renders exposed bright contact/scope surfaces and crowded tablet/wide hero geometry. Those surfaces were migrated to dark materials, the architecture was resized/repositioned, tablet space was increased, and a decorative annotation colliding with the BREAKS period was removed. The final large identity print now shares the custom uppercase vector RH. The range input has a 44px hit area.

The final hero was visually inspected at 360, 390, 768, 1024, 1440, and 1920px. All nine routes have phone/desktop captures and 54 route/viewport layout analyses without overflow. Native keyboard, mouse drag, emulated touch, layer focus, section inspection, reduced-motion cleanup, and actual entrance/request trace frames are verified. Current executed commands, accessibility results, performance measurements, and practical limits are recorded in [verification.md](verification.md).
