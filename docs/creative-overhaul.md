# Creative audit and overhaul

Historical audit of the earlier paper-direction overhaul. The current dark architectural refinement is documented in [dark-refinement.md](dark-refinement.md).

## Audit before implementation

The running production homepage and research index were inspected in Chromium at 1440px and the homepage at 390px. Before screenshots are preserved in `.qa/overhaul/before/`.

The old design was functional but compositionally predictable: a left headline/right diagram hero, equal three-card archive, repeated two-line headings with right-side summaries, alternating dark/light blocks, and small schematic artwork. Its motif never changed the visitor's understanding of the system. The meaningful interactive explorer was buried below several conventional sections. Thin typography, conservative scale changes, and identical spacing gave every subject nearly the same weight. Mobile compressed that rhythm into a long, uniform stack. These weaknesses would limit originality and interaction scores in a serious creative review.

## Awarded reference comparison

- [NEVERHACK](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/neverhack): Awwwards confirms Site of the Day on 3 June 2025 and a Developer Award. Inspected its original submitted screenshot through Awwwards, including the dominant volumetric object, integrated copy, and precise perimeter framing. Also rendered its current live homepage, which has changed substantially from that submission; its current design is not treated as the awarded version.
- [Anime.js](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/%2399F4C0/): Awwwards' listing records Developer Award and Site of the Month in May 2025. Rendered its live homepage and used browser scrolling. Its evolving central object demonstrates the product instead of functioning as unrelated decoration; the concentration of visual energy is stronger than the portfolio's old line diagram.
- [Eduard Bodak](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/%23f6f7f0/?page=1): Awwwards' listing records Site of the Day and Developer Award on 14 June 2025. Rendered the current live page. Its broad type, empty space, small peripheral navigation, and deliberately narrow work sequence create distinct spatial relationships. Some work media were unavailable, so those were not evaluated.

Live Igloo and Jordan Delcros pages were attempted but remained on loading screens in this environment; Bruno Simon did not yield a usable capture. No successful visual or interaction review is claimed for those attempts. Reference screenshots are in `.qa/overhaul/references/`. The comparisons identify principles, not assets or compositions to reproduce.

## New direction: Boundary Studies

Warm mineral paper, carbon ink, and an acid-yellow section plane. A mathematical engraved membrane gives invisible policy a physical presence. Large words act as architecture; fine annotations give scale. Research becomes a collection of unequal folios, not repeated cards. The same contour vocabulary changes meaning between the opening instrument, authorization, OAuth, code review, methodology, and the request experiment.

The sculpture is code-native vector artwork. Its separation and section controls expose its internal structure using pointer, touch, and native keyboard inputs. The request experiment evaluates a fictional caller/resource relationship and sharing grant, then draws the request's path through identity, policy, and resource. It illustrates the distinction between authentication and authorization; it does not contact or test any real system.

Motion shares one restrained physical language: translations, separation, a travelling request, and short editorial entries. Navigation remains native and content stays visible before enhancement. Reduced-motion and no-JavaScript paths remain first-class. Final renders, refinements, and measurements are recorded in the verification report after implementation.
